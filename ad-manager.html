Put it live

  1. Tap Copy ads.json.
  2. On GitHub, open your wayback repository and tap ads.json, then the pencil ✏️ to edit.
  3. Select everything in the file, delete it, and paste.
  4. Tap Commit changes. Phones show the new adverts within about 10 minutes of opening WakaBack.
